Why Corporate Tools?

Sometimes the people you learn from show you where you want to go next.

My introduction to Corporate Tools didn't begin with a job posting—it began in a classroom.

One of my Nucamp instructors works here. During class he saw the version of me that asks questions, volunteers to try things, and isn't afraid to admit when I don't know something yet.

That experience gave me a glimpse of the kind of people and culture that make Corporate Tools different. It made me realize this is the kind of team I want to grow with.

I still approach development the same way I approached that classroom: stay curious, ask questions, and never stop learning.
