Why Corporate Tools?

Sometimes the people you learn from show you where you want to go next.

My introduction to Corporate Tools didn't begin with a job posting—it began in a classroom.

One of my Nucamp instructors worked at Corporate Tools while he was teaching my cohort. Watching him teach gave me an appreciation for the curiosity, collaboration, and high standards that seemed to define the culture at Corporate Tools.

During class, he saw me ask questions, volunteer to take on challenges, and admit when I didn't know something yet. That experience confirmed that I thrive in environments where curiosity, collaboration, and continuous learning are encouraged.

That's why Corporate Tools stands out to me. I'm not just looking for my first front-end developer role—I'm looking for a team where I can continue growing, contribute meaningful work, and keep learning from the people around me.
